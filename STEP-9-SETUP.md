## Before launch
1. Replace `923000000000` with the real business WhatsApp number.
2. Replace the placeholder email.
3. Replace `YOUR-DOMAIN.example` in SEO/robots/sitemap files.
4. Add final delivery charges, service areas, return policy and support hours.
5. Add real product names, descriptions, PKR prices, stock and photos through the admin dashboard.
6. Add the SEO snippet to the home page `<head>`.
7. Add `<script src="whatsapp.js"></script>` before `</body>` where desired.
8. Add footer links to Contact, Delivery & Returns, Privacy, My Account and Cart.

## Product catalog standard
Use a clear name, useful description, PKR price, stock quantity, consistent photos,
and size/color/delivery details where relevant.

## Pakistan checkout
Keep Cash on Delivery if it fits the business. Clearly show delivery charges and service areas.
Do not advertise an online payment method until its real gateway/account process is configured.

## WhatsApp
Use WhatsApp for support and product questions. Prefer website checkout for actual orders so every order
is recorded in the admin dashboard.

## Note
This package does not invent real product names, prices or photos. Those should be added through the admin dashboard.
